Questions we hear

Straight answers before you call.

Here's what construction companies ask us most, from pricing to who writes the words.

01Do we need to meet in person?

No. We start with a conversation, whether it's a call, an email, or in person, and handle everything from there.

02How much does a website cost?

Foundation websites start at $2,999 for five custom pages with copywriting included. Premier websites, built on WordPress so you can edit them yourself, start at $5,999. Additional pages are $299 on Foundation and $399 on Premier.

03How long does a website take?

Most Foundation websites launch in about 30 days once we have your photos, logo, and story in hand. When you need it sooner, our rush launch has you live within one week for $1,000.

04Do you write the words, or do we?

We do. We learn your history, your people, and the legacy you carry forward, then we write every page.

05Will our current site go down during the switch?

No. Your current site stays live until the new one is ready.

06Who owns the website?

You do. Every page, photo, and file we create belongs to you.

07What happens after launch?

Our care plans keep it current. Our team handles hosting, backups, security, and monthly edits, starting at $99 a month.
